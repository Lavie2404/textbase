"Vô Danh Lục" is a solo, non-commercial Vietnamese-language interactive novel
+ xianxia/tu tiên RPG. Engine Godot 4.6 GDScript, target Web/Mobile Web.
Narrative and situations are dynamically generated by an LLM acting as an
"objective" game master. Player starts as a nobody ("vô danh") dropped into
an existing xianxia IP setting (Đấu La Đại Lục, Phàm Nhân Tu Tiên, Tiên
Nghịch, Kiếm Lai, etc.) as a new character — canon characters/events from
the source novel still occur "đúng như nguyên tác" at the macro level.

Core Fantasy (verbatim anchor, see [[feedback_adversarial-review-anchor]]):
"Bạn là một kẻ vô danh bước vào con đường tu luyện, trong một thế giới
không ban cho bạn bất kỳ đặc quyền nào." NPCs are meant to judge the player
purely on demonstrated strength/actions, never on protagonist status.

**Why this matters**: The entire premise depends on an LLM narrator refusing
to grant the player unearned narrative advantages (plot armor, convenient
rescues, favorable coincidences) — but LLMs are known to have a sycophancy/
protagonist-bias tendency from RLHF training. This is the single largest
structural risk to the stated Core Fantasy and, as of the 2026-08-01
adversarial review, had no explicit mechanical guardrail in the concept doc
(e.g., no rule forcing the AI narrator to only describe outcomes already
determined by the Lực chiến formula, with zero narrative discretion over
win/loss).

**How to apply**: Any future review or systems-design work on combat
resolution, NPC behavior, or the AI narration pipeline for this project
should check whether an explicit "AI narrator cannot alter formula-determined
outcomes" rule exists. If absent, flag it — it is the load-bearing guardrail
for pillars "Thế Giới Khách Quan" and "Sức Mạnh Có Logic".
